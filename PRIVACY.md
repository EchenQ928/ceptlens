# Privacy Notice

CeptLens processes only the data needed to provide learning, practice, discussion, and operational services. The public repository contains no real user profiles, account credentials, production SQLite databases, or model keys.

## Data categories

- Account and session data: registration, sign-in, identity binding, and security audit.
- Learning data: progress, saved questions, incorrect answers, assessments, and discussion content for cross-device learning and feedback.
- AI runtime data: model, time, status, latency, and usage information for service delivery and troubleshooting. API keys are not recorded.
- Operational data: health, error, and deployment records for reliability.

When the AI feature is enabled, questions, selected text, and prompts submitted by a user may be sent to the configured model service. Production operators must review that provider's data policy and avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Users may request access to, export of, or deletion of their account and learning data. Deletion may retain de-identified security audit records for safety and troubleshooting. Beta retention periods and contact details are defined by the deployment environment.
